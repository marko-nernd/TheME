MODEL ESCAPE — FIVE-PERSON USABILITY CHECK

Purpose
Check whether new people understand booking, payment, and event entry without coaching.
Use localhost, test accounts, Stripe test cards, and fictional event data only.
Do not collect real identity documents, card details, bank details, or private messages.

Who to invite
Two models, two photographers, and one host who have not helped build the site.
Recruiting people and arranging sessions remains a task for the project owner.
Plan 15–20 minutes per person. Ask permission before taking notes or recording.

Opening script
“We are testing the website, not you. Please say what you expect to happen as you
go. I will avoid helping so we can discover what is unclear. No real payment is made.”

Model and photographer tasks
1. Sign in and explain what needs your attention on Home.
2. Find an event for your account type. Explain the event price, optional stay,
   service fee, and total before paying.
3. Complete a test payment. Find the confirmation code and QR event pass.
4. Add the event to a calendar and find directions and what to bring.
5. Find the payment receipt and separate accommodation charge.
6. Find the refund cutoff and explain the difference between canceling attendance
   and requesting a refund. Do not issue a refund during the research session.
7. Open Inbox, identify a conversation, and find Profile and Billing again.

Host tasks
1. Open Manage events and choose the correct event.
2. Find confirmed participants, people awaiting payment, and event proceeds.
3. Check in a fictional attendee using the six-character code.
4. Try the same code again. Explain what the duplicate warning means.
5. Locate a refund request and describe the decision before pressing any button.
6. Find test payout setup and explain where event proceeds and service fees go.
   Do not enter real bank or identity information.

Initial acceptance targets (not measured results)
At least four of five participants find the main next action without help.
All understand the total charge before payment.
At least four of five find their event pass within 30 seconds after confirmation.
All understand that attendance cancellation is not an automatic refund.
The host recognizes a duplicate check-in and does not admit twice.
No participant mistakes optional membership upgrades or ID verification for an
extra requirement to book an otherwise available event.

After the sessions
Group issues by repeated confusion, financial risk, and accessibility impact.
Fix payment misunderstandings and blocked tasks first. Retest those exact tasks.
Keep personal opinions separate from observed failures. Do not claim the flow
has passed this study until the sessions have actually taken place.

Release checks still requiring owner action
- Enable Stripe Connect in TEST mode and complete a host's test onboarding.
- Confirm the connected account's transfer and bank-payout capabilities.
- Verify a destination test payment and reversal using Stripe's test tools.
- Configure and verify signed webhook delivery for success, failure, and refunds.
- Test supported camera scanning on a physical phone over HTTPS; localhost on
  another device is not the Mac's localhost. Manual confirmation codes remain available.
- Decide live-country availability, taxes, disputes, processor-fee refunds, and
  platform liability before any live payment launch.
- Replace local Mailpit delivery with a verified email service before production.
